Version 1.0, 2026-10-31.

You do not have to contribute to ITSaNAS. If you do, this agreement applies.
It gives the project everything in your contribution. **It gives you nothing
in return, and the project owes you nothing**: not that your contribution will
be used, not credit, not payment, not any particular licence. Unless a later
agreement signed by the Maintainer says otherwise, there is no other deal. If
that is not acceptable to you, do not contribute.

## 8. How You agree

You agree, for each Contribution You submit to the project from then on and
for each one submitted before, by doing either of the following:

- opening a pull request whose description contains this sentence:

  > I have read the ITSaNAS Contributor Agreement (CLA.md, version 1.0) and I agree to it.

- or submitting any Contribution after this agreement was published in the
  project's repository.

The project's `CLA` check refuses a pull request without the sentence. The
agreement is recorded by the pull request and its history.
